PA

A private, self-hosted personal assistant for one household.

PA runs on its owner's own server at home. It reads its owner's email and a few other accounts to cut through the noise: it summarizes what needs attention, highlights school updates, and suggests mail filters and unsubscribes. It sends a daily summary to its owner.

PA is not a public service. There is no sign-up, and it is used only by the household that runs it. Nothing it reads is sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Google account access

PA asks for read access to its owner's Gmail so it can sort and summarize mail. Email is processed on the owner's own hardware, including by a language model running locally, and is not sent to outside AI services. Any change to the mailbox (applying a label, creating a filter, unsubscribing) happens only after the owner approves it.
