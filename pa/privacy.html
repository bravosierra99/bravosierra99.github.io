Privacy policy

PA: a private, self-hosted personal assistant. Last updated 26 October 2026.

Who uses PA

PA is used only by the household that runs it, on its own server. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What PA accesses

  • Gmail (when its owner grants access): message headers and content, labels, and filter settings, so it can summarize mail, flag what needs attention, and suggest filters and unsubscribes.
  • Other accounts the owner connects, such as school grade portals, read-only.

How the data is used

  • Data is processed only to provide PA's features to its owner: summaries, alerts, suggestions, and a daily digest delivered to the owner's own inbox.
  • Processing happens on the owner's own hardware, including a language model that runs locally. Email content is not sent to third-party AI services and is not used to train any model.
  • PA changes the mailbox (labels, filters, unsubscribes) only with the owner's approval. It does not send email on the owner's behalf except a daily summary to the owner.

Storage and retention

  • Data is stored on the owner's own server, on the home network, and in encrypted backups that only the owner can decrypt.
  • Full message content is kept for at most 30 days; after that only derived information (for example, "this sender sends 12 promotions a month") is kept.
  • Access credentials are stored only on that server.

Sharing

PA does not sell, rent, or share data with anyone, and shows no ads.

Google API Services

PA's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can revoke PA's access to a Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. When that happens, PA stops reading mail, and its stored data can be deleted from the server.
